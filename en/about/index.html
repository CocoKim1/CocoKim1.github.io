ABOUT / COCO KIM

Fourteen months on the road,
revisited one story at a time

Hello, I’m Coco Kim.

I left South Korea in my own car and travelled for roughly fourteen months through more than forty countries, reaching The Gambia in West Africa.

Here I am documenting the border crossings, nights in the car, long drives and encounters that could not all fit into the YouTube videos, using the original photographs and notes.

Editorial principle

Countries, cities, dates and experiences are published only after they are verified against the original archive.
