01 / THE JOURNEY

The drive is over. Now the full story begins.

This was not a sequence of flights and city breaks. I carried daily life in my own car and spent fourteen months crossing borders by road.

This site is an archive in progress, rebuilding the journey across more than forty countries from the original photographs, videos and notes.
